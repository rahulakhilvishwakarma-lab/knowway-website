7. Location-Based Features & Route Planning Disclaimer

When you grant location permission, KnowWay uses your GPS coordinates (and optionally your manual stay location or selected transit hubs such as airports and railway stations) to discover nearby places by radius, compute geodesic distances, snap waypoints to roads, and estimate road routes via OpenStreetMap-based OSRM routing, Nominatim reverse geocoding, Overpass POI queries, and Carto/Leaflet map rendering.

Route distances, drive times, corridor suggestions, and Trip Cart GPS arrival checks (which verify proximity within configured GPS accuracy and radius thresholds) are planning and tracking aids only. Turn-by-turn navigation launches external third-party map applications (such as Google Maps or Apple Maps). Always obey traffic laws, local signage, road barriers, and real-time road conditions.
