Privacy Policy
How KnowWay Collects, Uses, Stores & Protects Information
Effective October 2026 · Last updated November 2026

At KnowWay, we respect your privacy and are committed to transparent, responsible handling of personal information. This Privacy Policy describes what data is processed when you use the KnowWay website (https://knowway.in), Progressive Web App (PWA), and Android mobile application (com.knowway.india).

This document reflects KnowWay’s actual technical architecture and permissions. Please read it carefully alongside our Terms & Conditions.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all visitors, travelers, and registered users accessing KnowWay across desktop web browsers, mobile web browsers, installed PWAs, and the Android mobile application.

It covers account authentication, location-powered discovery, route planning, AI translation and voice journey features, push notifications, banner ads on Android, analytics events, local device storage, and account deletion.

2. Data Fiduciary / Platform Identity

KnowWay operates the travel discovery platform accessible at https://knowway.in and via the KnowWay Android application.

For personal data processed under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), KnowWay acts as the Data Fiduciary for data collected directly through our interfaces. You can reach us regarding privacy matters at support@knowway.in.

3. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use KnowWay, we collect and process only the categories of data required for the features you engage with:

  • Account & Profile Information: If you sign in with Google or Email OTP via Supabase Auth, we receive your authentication user ID, email address, display name, and profile avatar URL (where provided by Google). If you voluntarily update your profile, we also store optional fields you enter such as mobile number, gender, date of birth, city, state, and bio.
  • Trip Cart, Favourites & Journey Verification Data: Saved favourite place IDs, Trip Cart itineraries (route stops, mode, distances), completed trip IDs, and—when you verify arriving at a stop—the stop ID, timestamp, GPS coordinates, and GPS accuracy meters.
  • Location Coordinates: Your device GPS coordinates (when location permission is granted) or manually entered stay coordinates used to discover nearby attractions, calculate distances, and map routes.
  • Voice Journey Text Queries: When using the Voice Journey Assistant, your device/browser speech recognition converts spoken words into text, and the text prompt is sent to our server to parse travel intent. KnowWay does not record or store raw voice audio files on its servers.
  • Device, Push Notification & Usage Analytics Data: Client-generated device and session identifiers, platform type (e.g. desktop_web, mobile_web, pwa_android, android_native), browser user agent, selected language, feature usage events (such as page views, place views, audio plays, route starts, and install prompt funnels), and—on Android when notifications are enabled—your Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token, device model, app version, timezone, and notification category preferences.
  • Account Deletion Portal Records: When submitting a web deletion request, your email address, optional full name/reason, verification token, user agent, and a truncated SHA-256 hash of your request IP address.

Note on Bookings: KnowWay’s database includes a bookings schema for administrative compatibility, but user-facing booking and affiliate purchase UI is currently disabled (BOOKING_FEATURES_ENABLED = false) and KnowWay collects no bank, card, or UPI payment details.

4. How Information Is Collected

We collect information through four mechanisms verified in our application:

  • Direct User Input: When you sign in, edit your profile, save trips, type a journey query, or submit an account deletion request.
  • Device & Browser Permissions: When you grant optional OS or browser permissions for Location (GPS), Microphone (for Voice Journey speech-to-text), or Android Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS).
  • Automated Technical & Usage Events: First-party session and feature-interaction events sent to KnowWay’s analytics endpoint and Supabase backend to measure usage and diagnose errors.
  • Local Browser/Device Storage: Preferences, cached sessions, and offline assets saved in localStorage, sessionStorage, and PWA cache.

5. How We Use Information (Purpose Limitation)

We use collected information strictly to operate, maintain, and improve KnowWay’s travel features:

  • Discovery & Routing: Finding attractions near your location or chosen city, calculating distances, optimizing stop order, and generating road polylines.
  • Account Sync & Trip Planning: Authenticating sessions, syncing your profile, saving Trip Carts, and verifying completed itinerary stops.
  • Multilingual & Voice Features: Translating attraction descriptions into 10 Indian languages and parsing natural-language trip requests.
  • Notifications & Reliability: Delivering opted-in Android travel reminders via FCM, enforcing API rate limits, preventing spam, and monitoring platform health.

6. Location Data & Permissions

KnowWay requests optional location access (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION on Android, or browser navigator.geolocation on web) to power "Near Me" discovery, distance badges, stay-area filtering, and route start points.

Coordinates are evaluated on-device for radius filtering and distance sorting, and—only when needed for routing, reverse geocoding, or Trip Cart stop-arrival verification—sent to routing/geocoding services (OSRM, Nominatim) or KnowWay’s stop-verification endpoint (/api/journey/complete-stop). KnowWay does not perform silent background GPS tracking when the app is unused.

You may deny or turn off location access at any time via your browser permissions or Android App Settings (accessible from More -> App Settings -> Location Permission). Manual State/City discovery remains fully usable without GPS.

7. Cookies, Local Storage, Session Storage & Caching

KnowWay does not set first-party document.cookie tracking cookies in our application code. Instead, KnowWay relies on modern browser storage and Progressive Web App caching:

  • localStorage: Stores Supabase authentication session tokens (sb-*-auth-token), random device ID (knowway_device_id), selected UI language, audio speed/voice preferences, saved place IDs, local profile/Trip Cart caches, completed stops, notification preferences, Android FCM token state, and first-launch flags.
  • sessionStorage: Stores ephemeral session ID (knowway_session_id), pending trip drafts prior to login, and active filter/map context across page reloads during a browsing session.
  • PWA Service Worker Cache: Uses Workbox runtime caching to store static app shell files, Google Fonts, Leaflet assets, OpenStreetMap tiles, and viewed Wikimedia/Unsplash/Supabase attraction images for faster offline/repeat loading.

Note: Third-party services integrated into KnowWay (such as Google OAuth sign-in pages, Google AdMob SDK, or external CDNs) may set or read their own standard cookies or identifiers subject to their respective privacy policies.

8. AI Processing & Automated Features (Google Gemini API)

KnowWay uses the Google Gemini API (@google/genai) on our backend server strictly for specific functional tasks:

  • Attraction Translation (/api/translate): Public attraction text and your chosen target language code are sent to Gemini to generate regional-language translations (cached on the server).
  • Coordinate Lookup & Enrichment (/api/places/lookup-coordinates): Attraction names and state/district details are sent to Gemini to verify coordinates and prepare historical summaries.
  • Voice Journey Intent Parsing (/api/voice/parse-journey): The text transcription of your spoken or typed journey request is sent to Gemini to extract city, duration, and travel interest filters.

Please avoid speaking or entering sensitive personal information (such as financial details, Aadhaar/government IDs, or private addresses) into Voice Journey fields. Audio narration ("Read Aloud") runs locally via Android Text-to-Speech or browser Web Speech API without sending user text to Gemini.

9. Third-Party Infrastructure, APIs & Service Providers

To deliver KnowWay across web and Android, data may be transmitted to or processed by the following verified technical providers:

  • Supabase: Provides authentication (Google Sign-In & Email OTP), PostgreSQL database storage, Row-Level Security enforcement, attraction media storage (knowway-attractions bucket), and serverless Edge Functions (delete-account, send-push-notification).
  • Vercel: Hosts the production web frontend and executes Express serverless API routes (/api/*), maintaining standard HTTP edge and serverless execution logs.
  • GitHub: Used exclusively for source code management, CI/CD automated Android APK and web builds, and syncing the public tourist attraction catalog. GitHub is not used to store end-user personal profiles or private account data.
  • Google Services: Google Sign-In / Credential Manager (authentication), Google Gemini API (translation/research/voice parsing), Google AdMob (Android banner ads and consent prompts), Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android push notifications), Google Fonts CDN, and external Google Maps navigation links.
  • Open Mapping & Image CDNs: Carto basemap tiles, OpenStreetMap OSRM (routing), Nominatim (reverse geocoding), Overpass API (nearby amenity queries), Leaflet CDN, Wikimedia Commons, Unsplash, and external Apple Maps links.

10. Advertising, Sponsorships & Affiliate Disclosures

On Android devices, KnowWay integrates the Google AdMob SDK (@capacitor-community/admob) to display banner ads. Before requesting ads, KnowWay invokes AdMob’s consent check (requestConsentInfo / showConsentForm) where applicable.

Third-party travel affiliate/booking cards present in the codebase (such as cab, hotel, train, or bus partner links) are currently turned off in the live interface. If promotional links or affiliate offers are activated in future releases, clicking those links directs you to external partner platforms governed by their own privacy notices.

11. How We Share Information

KnowWay does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or saved itineraries to third parties.

Information is disclosed only: (a) to the infrastructure, mapping, AI, notification, and advertising providers listed in Section 9 solely as needed to deliver the Service; or (b) when required by applicable Indian law, court order, or lawful government/regulatory request.

12. Data Storage, Cross-Border Transfers & Security Measures

Account records, user profiles, push tokens, and usage analytics are stored in Supabase PostgreSQL cloud databases. In Express server deployments with a writable filesystem, Trip Cart and stop-verification snapshots are also maintained in a server-side trip store file (data/user_trips_store.json) alongside Supabase auth user metadata.

We apply practical safeguards including HTTPS/TLS transport encryption, Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) policies, JWT Bearer authentication checks on user and admin APIs, server-side rate limiting, input/URL XSS sanitization, and SHA-256 IP hashing on deletion requests.

However, no internet transmission, cloud host, or browser storage mechanism is 100% immune to security risks, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Data Retention & Account Deletion Disclosures

You can request permanent deletion of your KnowWay account at any time inside the app (More -> Delete Account) or via https://knowway.in/delete-account.

When an account deletion request is verified and executed:

  • Immediately Deleted Data: Your Supabase Auth account records (auth.users and user_metadata), user profiles (knowway_user_profiles), user roles, personal user comments, error reports, and registered device push tokens are deleted upon execution, and local browser storage (localStorage/sessionStorage) is cleared.
  • Anonymized & Redacted Data: Analytics sessions, events, and app installation records are disassociated from user IDs; and legacy bookings rows have customer PII redacted.
  • Temporarily Retained Data & Provider Backup Cycles: Server-side JSON trip store backups (data/user_trips_store.json), deletion audit logs, and hosting/database infrastructure backups managed by Vercel and Supabase are not purged instantaneously upon user request and remain subject to standard provider-defined hosting and database automated backup and log retention schedules.

14. Your Privacy Rights & Choices (DPDP Act & Rule 7 Breach Notification)

Under applicable Indian data protection law (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules), you have rights to access, correction, consent withdrawal, erasure, and grievance redressal through our designated Grievance Officer, Akhil Vishwakarma.

Personal Data Breach Notification (DPDP Rule 7): In the event of a personal data breach, KnowWay adheres to Rule 7: notifying affected Data Principals and the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) without undue delay upon breach discovery, with detailed Board information furnished within 72 hours, subject to phased statutory commencement dates.

  • Access & Correction: View and update profile details directly inside KnowWay or contact us for assistance.
  • Withdraw Consent & Permissions: Turn Location, Microphone, or Notification permissions ON/OFF at any time in device/browser settings.
  • Erasure & Deletion: Delete your account in-app or submit a verified deletion request at https://knowway.in/delete-account.
  • Grievance Officer Name: Akhil Vishwakarma
  • Grievance Redressal: Write to support@knowway.in with any privacy concern, data correction request, or grievance.

15. Children’s Privacy

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, individuals under 18 years of age are classified as children requiring verifiable parental or guardian consent for personal data processing. KnowWay is not intended for children under 18 to create independent accounts or submit personal data without parental/guardian involvement, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 anywhere.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal data to KnowWay without appropriate consent, please contact us at support@knowway.in so we can review and remove the information.

16. International Data Transfers

Although KnowWay focuses on Indian travel discovery, our cloud, AI, mapping, notification, and advertising partners (such as Supabase, Vercel, Google Gemini/AdMob/FCM, and OpenStreetMap services) operate infrastructure globally. Accordingly, information processed through KnowWay may be transmitted to and stored on servers outside India in accordance with those providers’ technical safeguards and applicable Indian law.

17. Push Notifications & Device Permissions

On Android, KnowWay declares optional permissions in AndroidManifest.xml strictly for user-initiated features: INTERNET (required for maps, data, and APIs), ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION (optional GPS discovery), RECORD_AUDIO (optional microphone input for Voice Journey), and POST_NOTIFICATIONS (optional journey reminders).

If you enable Journey Notifications while signed in on Android, KnowWay registers an FCM device token linked to your user ID and device ID along with your granular category toggles (trip cart reminders, planned journeys, travel updates, weekend trails, new destinations, follow-ups). Signing out or disabling Journey Notifications deactivates your device token.

18. External Websites & Third-Party Links

KnowWay contains external links to Google Maps, Apple Maps, Wikimedia Commons image source pages, Unsplash, official heritage references, and support channels. Once you leave KnowWay, we have no control over those external websites or apps, and their own privacy policies apply.

19. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as KnowWay evolves or as Indian privacy regulations (such as DPDP Rules) take effect. Any updates will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of KnowWay after updates take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised Terms.
