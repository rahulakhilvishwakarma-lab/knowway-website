A little about Knowway

Built around your curiosity.

Knowway is an India-focused travel and heritage discovery project built around a simple idea: you should be able to explore places and plan journeys your way.

Our purpose

Help travellers discover more than the obvious destination. From nearby attractions to cultural landmarks and route-side places, Knowway brings discovery and trip organization into one travel companion. Information and travel conditions can change, so visitors should verify opening hours, access and local guidance before travelling.
