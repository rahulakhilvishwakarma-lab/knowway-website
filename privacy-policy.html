1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Knowway (“Knowway”, “we”, “our” or “us”) may handle information when you visit knowway.in or use the Knowway application. Knowway is an India-focused travel and heritage discovery and planning service. We aim to use information only where it is relevant to operating, securing and improving the service.

2. Information and permissions

The information involved can depend on which features you use and the permissions you grant:

  • Account information: if you sign in, information made available through the sign-in provider, such as your name, email address and profile image, may be processed to identify your account. Google sign-in is used by the app where enabled.
  • Location: if you allow location access and use location-based features, the app may use your device location to help find nearby attractions or support route-related features. You can manage location permission through your device settings. Denying permission may limit location-dependent features.
  • Places and trip selections: places you choose, saved items, trip selections, favourites or related content may be processed to provide the corresponding features, depending on the app functionality you use and whether you are signed in.
  • Messages and feedback: if you contact us or submit a suggestion, report or feedback, we receive the details you choose to send, such as your message and any contact information you include.
  • Technical information: hosting, security and service infrastructure may process technical logs such as request time, device/browser information, IP address or error diagnostics. The exact logs depend on the services and configuration in use.

Please do not send sensitive personal information in feedback or place suggestions unless it is necessary to explain your request.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to provide sign-in and account features; display or save your selected places; support nearby discovery and route-planning functions; respond to feedback and support requests; protect the service from misuse; diagnose errors; and maintain or improve the app and website. We do not describe these purposes as a promise to collect every listed category in every session—the information processed depends on the features you use.

4. Services and external links

Some functions may rely on service providers or external platforms—for example, sign-in, cloud hosting/database, maps, routing, geocoding or external map links. Those providers may process the information needed to deliver their part of the service under their own terms and privacy notices. When you follow an external link, the external site or app's policies apply to your use of that service.

We do not sell personal information as a product. Information may be disclosed where needed to provide a requested service, protect users and the service, or comply with a valid legal obligation. The exact third-party provider list should be confirmed against the deployed app before this policy is finalised.

5. Storage, retention and security

Information may be stored on your device or in service infrastructure, depending on the feature. We aim to keep information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, for account and service operation, security, dispute handling or legal obligations. Retention periods can vary by data type and provider.

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device and account secure, and sign out on shared devices.

6. Your choices and requests

  • Manage location permissions in your device settings.
  • Choose whether to sign in, where the app supports use without an account.
  • Use the account controls available in the app for account management or deletion, if provided.
  • Contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information associated with your request. We may need to verify the request before acting.

Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for security and legitimate operational purposes. This policy does not promise immediate deletion from backups or third-party systems; requests will be handled according to applicable law and the relevant provider's capabilities.

7. Children

Knowway is a general travel discovery service. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of location-based and online services. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the concern.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, website, providers or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact goldenbirdin91@gmail.com. Please describe your request clearly and avoid sending passwords or other secret credentials.
