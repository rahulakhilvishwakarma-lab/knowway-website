Our purpose

Knowway is an India-focused travel and heritage discovery app built around one simple idea: a journey should fit the traveller, not the other way around. India has historic monuments, spiritual places, landscapes, local attractions and lesser-known stops spread across its states and districts. Knowway helps people discover places they may want to visit and bring their choices together into a more organised journey.

Our tagline, Your Journey, Your Rules., reflects that purpose. You decide what interests you, which places matter to you, and how you want to explore. Knowway is designed to support discovery and planning—not to tell every traveller to follow the same itinerary.

What Knowway helps you do

Nearby

Find attractions around your current area when you choose to use location features. Explore nearby options and decide what is worth a stop.

Bharatverse

Explore India's destinations and heritage across states and districts, with place information to help you decide what to discover next.

GoWay

Think beyond the destination. Corridor helps you look for interesting stops along a route; Layover is designed to help you consider places during a break between connections.

Trip Cart

Collect the places you choose in one place so you can review your selections and organise your trip ideas.

How we approach travel

We believe good travel planning starts with curiosity and clear information. Knowway brings place discovery and trip organisation together so that a monument, viewpoint, nature spot or unexpected stop can become part of a journey that feels personal to you.

We aim to make the experience straightforward: discover, compare, choose and plan. Place details, opening hours, fees, access conditions, road conditions and transport availability can change. Travellers should confirm important details with official or local sources before setting out.

What Knowway is—and is not

Knowway is a discovery and planning tool. Unless a feature explicitly says otherwise, it does not operate transport, guarantee admission, issue tickets, or guarantee that a place is open or accessible. Route suggestions and travel estimates are informational and should be used with your own judgement.

Knowway is being developed with a focus on useful discovery for India. Features and coverage may evolve over time; the app and its current screens are the best guide to what is available today.
