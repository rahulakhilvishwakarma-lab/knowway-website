HOW IT WORKS

Discover. Choose. Connect.

Four simple parts help you move from finding a place to shaping a journey.

On this page1. Discover2. Choose3. Connect with GoWay4. Organise your planBefore you travel

1. Discover places

Start with Nearby to explore attractions around you, or use Bharatverse to look across India's destinations and heritage. Browse the place information available in the app and shortlist the places that match your interests, time and travel style.

2. Choose what interests you

Not every trip needs a fixed checklist. Choose the monuments, historic sites, nature spots or other attractions you want to explore. You remain in control of which places make it into your plan.

3. Connect the journey with GoWay

Corridor is designed for finding interesting places along a route between an origin and a destination. It can help turn a direct journey into an opportunity to discover more. Layover is designed around a stopover: consider the time between arrival and departure, nearby options and whether a visit is realistic.

Travel time, traffic, opening hours, queues and connection requirements can change. Always leave a safe buffer and do not rely on an estimated visit time when missing a train, bus or flight would cause a problem.

4. Keep your selections in Trip Cart

Trip Cart brings your chosen places together so you can review your picks and shape your journey. It is intended to make your planning easier to follow, especially when you are considering several stops.

Before you travel

  • Confirm opening days, timings, entry rules and ticket prices with official sources.
  • Check the location and route before starting, especially in remote or unfamiliar areas.
  • Allow extra time for traffic, weather, parking, queues and security checks.
  • Follow local rules and respect heritage sites, residents and the natural environment.
  • Use current transport operator information for departure times and connection requirements.
Knowway supports planning; it cannot guarantee live availability, road conditions, opening hours, safety, or that every suggested stop will fit your schedule.
